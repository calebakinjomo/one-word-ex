Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **One Word From Your Ex**: exactly one word your ex would use to describe you. The funniest, most painfully honest word takes the whole reward.

The entry word:

> warmup

You weren't the main event. You were just the warmup act before the real thing showed up.

Run `python3 main.py` — it prints the entry word and writes it to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
